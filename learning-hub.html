About the Programme

A Professional Learning Experience for Development Practitioners

The KIID Africa Learning Hub is the capacity-building arm of Kingscity Initiative for Integrated Development (KIID). It delivers a structured, competency-based professional programme designed to strengthen the skills of development practitioners, NGO staff, researchers, M&E officers, project coordinators, and emerging professionals working across the humanitarian and development sector.

The programme bridges the gap between theory and practice. Rather than purely academic learning, participants engage with real-world case studies, hands-on assignments, and live mentorship — building a portfolio of work they can apply directly in their organisations and careers.

Who This Programme Is For

The Learning Hub is designed for anyone committed to building a career in development — from beginners exploring the sector to professionals seeking to sharpen their technical edge.

1
Beginner Track

For newcomers, NYSC members, and recent graduates entering the development sector.

2
Professional Track

For NGO staff, M&E officers, project managers, and researchers refining their practice.

3
Career Switchers

For professionals from other fields transitioning into development and humanitarian work.

4
Organisations

For NGOs and CSOs seeking to upskill their teams in MEAL, research, and project management.

Our Learning Approach

Learn by Doing, Not Just by Watching

The Learning Hub follows a competency-based model. Every week combines structured learning, practical application, and live mentorship — so participants build real skills, not just theoretical knowledge.

Structured Learning

Curated lessons, reading resources, and frameworks delivered weekly through a clear learning path.

Practical Application

Every concept is reinforced through hands-on assignments that mirror real development work.

Live Mentorship

Weekly review and Q&A sessions with experienced practitioners who guide your progress.

Portfolio Building

Participants complete a portfolio of work products they can present to employers and partners.

Programme Structure

Six Phases, Twelve Weeks

The programme is organised into six progressive phases. Each phase builds on the last, moving from foundations to advanced practice and culminating in a portfolio and capstone review.

1
Weeks 1–2

Foundations of Development Practice

Build a shared understanding of the development sector, key actors, frameworks, and the principles that guide responsible, ethical practice.

Development sector mapping Core principles & ethics Stakeholder ecosystems
2
Weeks 3–4

Project Management for Development

Learn the full project cycle — from needs assessment and design to implementation, monitoring, and closure — using sector-standard tools.

Project cycle management Logframes & budgets Work planning
3
Weeks 5–6

MEAL — Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning

Master the foundations of MEAL: indicators, data collection methods, accountability mechanisms, and how learning drives programme improvement.

MEAL frameworks Indicator design Accountability mechanisms
4
Weeks 7–8

Research & Data Collection

Develop practical research skills — qualitative and quantitative methods, tool design, fieldwork ethics, and data quality assurance.

Research design Data collection tools Fieldwork ethics
5
Weeks 9–10

Data Analytics & Visualisation

Turn raw data into insight. Learn to clean, analyse, and visualise data using accessible tools, and communicate findings for decision-making.

Data cleaning Analysis & visualisation Reporting for decisions
6
Weeks 11–12

Portfolio, Capstone & Career Pathways

Consolidate your learning into a professional portfolio, complete a capstone review, and map your next steps in the development sector.

Portfolio finalisation Capstone review Career pathway planning
Weekly Learning Schedule

A Rhythm That Builds Mastery

Learn
