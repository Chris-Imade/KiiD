Invitation to Join Cohort 2: Enumerators & Research Assistants Training | KIID

Kingscity Initiative for Integrated Development (KIID) invites you to register for Cohort 2 of its Enumerators and Research Assistants Training. This training equips participants with practical, field-ready data collection skills for NGO, donor-funded, UN, and academic research projects.

Who Should Join

Graduates, students, researchers, MEAL officers, field officers, and development practitioners who want to strengthen research skills and employability.

Why You Should Join

Participants will gain:

  • Practical skills in surveys, FGDs, and KIIs
  • Understanding of research ethics, safeguarding, and consent
  • Knowledge of field protocols and data quality standards
  • Hands-on role plays and field simulations
  • Readiness for baseline, endline, and rapid assessments
  • Free KRAT Research Manual
  • Opportunity to join the Elite KIID Research and Assessment Team (KRAT)

This training focuses on real-world application to build confident enumerators.
